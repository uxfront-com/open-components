Each component's rules that rest on WCAG 2.2, WAI-ARIA or the APG are now on a page of their own, so you can check a component's accessibility without reading all four of its layers. We build each list from the component's checklists, so it always says what they say.

## New

- Each of the 17 components has an accessibility checklist, under Accessibility Checklists in the sidebar.
- Every row names the WCAG 2.2 success criteria behind its rule, with their level, and links each one to its Understanding page.
- Every rule links back to its table on the component's page, where we explain why it's there.
