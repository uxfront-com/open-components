The Components section now has a page of its own, so you can see every component the standard covers in one place, and go straight to the layer you need. It's the first item under Components in the sidebar.

## New

- The page lists every component, grouped by what it's for, with a sentence on what each one does.
- Each component links to its Overview and to its UI, UX, DX and AX pages.
- Agents read the same list in the page's markdown, and through our MCP server.
