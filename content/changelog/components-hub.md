The Components section now has an Overview of its own, so you can see every component the standard covers in one place, and go straight to the layer you need. It's the first item under Components in the sidebar.

## New

- Each component has a card, grouped by what it's for, with a drawing of it and a sentence on what it does.
- A card's name opens the component's Overview, and its footer links its UI, UX, DX and AX pages.
- Agents read the same links in the page's markdown, and through our MCP server.
