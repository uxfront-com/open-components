Open Components is an open standard for UI components, and this page lists the guidelines for every component it covers. Each one walks you through building a component that looks right, works for everyone, feels familiar to developers and can be checked by your agents. The guidelines are free to use, share and adapt under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as long as you credit us.

Every component has an Overview, with its At a glance table, its checklist and a tested reference implementation, then a page per layer. The User Interface (UI) is how it looks, the User Experience (UX) is how it behaves for every person and input, the Developer Experience (DX) is how developers use it, and the Agentic Experience (AX) is how agents read, build and check it.

If you're new here, the [Button](/docs/components/button) is the best place to start, since it sets the sizes, colours and states the other components share. The [Roadmap](/docs/getting-started/roadmap) lists the components we're writing next.

## Actions and forms

These are the components people use to act and to fill in forms:

- The [Button](/docs/components/button) lets people act on what's in front of them, from saving a form to deleting a file. Read its [UI](/docs/components/button/ui), [UX](/docs/components/button/ux), [DX](/docs/components/button/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/button/ax).
- The [Field](/docs/components/field) labels a form control, with a hint and an error message when it needs them. Read its [UI](/docs/components/field/ui), [UX](/docs/components/field/ux), [DX](/docs/components/field/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/field/ax).
- The [Input](/docs/components/input) takes a single line of text, like a name, an email address or a search. Read its [UI](/docs/components/input/ui), [UX](/docs/components/input/ux), [DX](/docs/components/input/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/input/ax).
- The [Textarea](/docs/components/textarea) takes longer text, like a comment or a message. Read its [UI](/docs/components/textarea/ui), [UX](/docs/components/textarea/ux), [DX](/docs/components/textarea/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/textarea/ax).
- The [Checkbox](/docs/components/checkbox) lets people say yes or no to one thing, like agreeing to the terms. Read its [UI](/docs/components/checkbox/ui), [UX](/docs/components/checkbox/ux), [DX](/docs/components/checkbox/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/checkbox/ax).
- The [Checkbox Group](/docs/components/checkbox-group) lets people pick any number of options out of a few. Read its [UI](/docs/components/checkbox-group/ui), [UX](/docs/components/checkbox-group/ux), [DX](/docs/components/checkbox-group/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/checkbox-group/ax).
- The [Radio Group](/docs/components/radio-group) lets people pick one option out of a few, when they all fit on the screen. Read its [UI](/docs/components/radio-group/ui), [UX](/docs/components/radio-group/ux), [DX](/docs/components/radio-group/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/radio-group/ax).
- The [Switch](/docs/components/switch) flips a setting on or off, with an effect people see right away. Read its [UI](/docs/components/switch/ui), [UX](/docs/components/switch/ux), [DX](/docs/components/switch/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/switch/ax).
- The [Select](/docs/components/select) lets people pick one option, or several, out of many, from a list. Read its [UI](/docs/components/select/ui), [UX](/docs/components/select/ux), [DX](/docs/components/select/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/select/ax).
- The [Combobox](/docs/components/combobox) filters a list of suggestions as people type, like when they pick a country. Read its [UI](/docs/components/combobox/ui), [UX](/docs/components/combobox/ux), [DX](/docs/components/combobox/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/combobox/ax).
- The [Tag](/docs/components/tag) labels what something is about, like a topic or a picked value, and people can remove it or follow it. Read its [UI](/docs/components/tag/ui), [UX](/docs/components/tag/ux), [DX](/docs/components/tag/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/tag/ax).
- The [Tag Group](/docs/components/tag-group) shows a set of tags, like an issue's labels or a search's filters, that people can remove one by one. Read its [UI](/docs/components/tag-group/ui), [UX](/docs/components/tag-group/ux), [DX](/docs/components/tag-group/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/tag-group/ax).

## Overlays

These sit on top of the page, next to what opened them or in front of everything else:

- The [Dialog](/docs/components/dialog) asks for someone's full attention, and holds focus until they're done. Read its [UI](/docs/components/dialog/ui), [UX](/docs/components/dialog/ux), [DX](/docs/components/dialog/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/dialog/ax).
- The [Popover](/docs/components/popover) shows more next to what opened it, without blocking the rest of the page. Read its [UI](/docs/components/popover/ui), [UX](/docs/components/popover/ux), [DX](/docs/components/popover/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/popover/ax).
- The [Tooltip](/docs/components/tooltip) gives a short hint on hover and focus, like an icon-only button's label. Read its [UI](/docs/components/tooltip/ui), [UX](/docs/components/tooltip/ux), [DX](/docs/components/tooltip/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/tooltip/ax).

## Feedback and display

These tell people what's going on, or group what they need to know about one thing:

- The [Spinner](/docs/components/spinner) shows that something is busy, for a wait of unknown length. Read its [UI](/docs/components/spinner/ui), [UX](/docs/components/spinner/ux), [DX](/docs/components/spinner/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/spinner/ax).
- The [Card](/docs/components/card) groups related content, like a project or a product, in one container. Read its [UI](/docs/components/card/ui), [UX](/docs/components/card/ux), [DX](/docs/components/card/dx) and [AX](/docs/components/card/ax).
